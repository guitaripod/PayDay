# Pay Day ASO — September 2026 revision (shipped with 1.1.0)

Why: the 2026-07 localized listing went live with 1.0.2/1.0.3 and moved the
Finnish cluster to the top (`FI/verkkolasku` #1, `FI/e-lasku` #2, `DE/peppol`
#5) but Belgium — the one market whose B2B mandate is already in force —
stayed flat (`BE/peppol` #28, `BE/e-factuur` / `e-facturatie` unranked), and
downloads sat at 7 in August and 3 in the first half of September while
France's receive obligation went live on 1 September. Zero ratings, raw
unframed screenshots, and no English (U.K.) localization were the three
structural gaps. Baseline ranks for 2026-09-16 are in
`~/Dev/operator/reports/aso-ranks.csv` (`aso-rank.py`).

## What changed

### 1. English (U.K.) listing — the cross-indexing lever
Apple indexes **English (U.K.)** as an additional localization in every EU
storefront (BE, FR, DE, NL, FI, AT, CH, LU, SE, DK, NO, PL) and as the primary
one in IE and GB. Pay Day had no en-GB, so those storefronts indexed nothing
beyond the native locale. en-GB now carries a second title/subtitle/keyword
set aimed at the mandate terms the native locales cannot spare room for:

- Name `Pay Day: Peppol E-Invoicing` · Subtitle `EN 16931 Factur-X UBL invoices`
- Keywords `e-factuur,e-facturatie,e-rechnung,xrechnung,verkkolasku,e-lasku,facture,sole,trader,vat,ubl,ireland`

Screenshots and description are the en-US set.

`pflicht` + `e-rechnung` (title) targets `e-rechnung pflicht`, which returns a
single app in DE. `belgique` / `België` put the Belgian mandate into the two
locales the Belgian storefront actually indexes.

### 3. Description + promotional text: the mandate is current
Paragraph two now states the live facts (Belgium in force with fines, France
receive obligation live since 1 September 2026 with SMEs issuing from 1
September 2027, Germany 2027/2028, EU-wide 2030) in all 14 locales, plus a
bullet for the new dashboard card. Promotional text leads with Belgium and
France.

### 4. Screenshots are framed and captioned
`scripts/capture-screenshots.sh` drives the DEBUG `PAYDAY_DEMO` routes per
locale (with `PAYDAY_DEMO_COUNTRY` pinning the dashboard card to the market
each locale sells into) and `scripts/frame-screenshots.swift` composes the
1320×2868 App Store frames from `marketing/captions/<locale>.json`. Screenshot
1 is the compliance hero; screenshot 3 is the dashboard with the country's
mandate card.

### 5. Product changes that feed the loop (1.1.0)
- Dashboard e-invoicing card (`MandateCopy` / `MandateCardView`) with the
  seller's country regime and a CTA into the paywall or Peppol setup.
- Welcome-screen hook with the device region's deadline.
- Rate / Share rows in Settings; rating prompt after the 2nd delivered invoice.
